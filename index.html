    Help & good to know

    Static, so it never expires

    These QR codes contain your information directly. They work forever, cost nothing and cannot be switched off. The trade-off is that you can’t change the link later or count scans. If you need either, print a short link you control instead.

    Private by design

    Everything is made in your browser. Your text, photos and logos are never uploaded. Saved designs live in your browser’s storage only.

    Make it scan every time

    • Dark pattern on a light background scans best.
    • Keep the logo at 22% or less.
    • Shorter links make simpler, easier codes.
    • Print at least 2 cm (¾ in) wide. Test with a phone first.
    • Watch the scan check under the preview.

    Shortcuts

    • ?url=https://… after the address pre-fills a link.
    • “Copy design link” shares your style (not your logo) with anyone.
    • Install it as an app from your browser menu to use it offline.
